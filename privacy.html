Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Cutroom is operated by Jesse Turner ("we", "us") in the United Kingdom. This page says what information Cutroom handles when you connect a social media account, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account connection. When you connect a TikTok account through TikTok's login, TikTok gives Cutroom an access token, your open id, and your basic profile: display name and avatar. That is all Cutroom asks for.
  • Videos you post. The clip files you choose to post, and the title or caption you give them.
  • Post records. After a post, the link to it and the time it was made, so the app can show you what has been posted and avoid posting the same clip twice.

What we do not collect

Cutroom does not read your messages, comments, followers, watch history or anyone else's data. It does not collect payment details, location, contacts or device identifiers. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How it is used

Only to do what you asked: connect the account you chose and post the clips you chose to it. Cutroom does not post without your action, does not sell or share your information with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Everything above is stored on the operator's own computers in the United Kingdom, in files readable only by the operator. Access tokens are stored so you do not have to log in every time. Nothing is sent to third parties, except the platform you are posting to and, for Instagram only, a short-lived file host that the platform fetches the video from.

How long it is kept

Access tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or they expire. Post records are kept for as long as the app is in use. Clip files are kept until they are no longer needed for posting.

Your choices

You can disconnect Cutroom at any time from your TikTok settings (Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services), which invalidates the token. You can ask us to delete anything Cutroom holds about your account by emailing jesseturnerjjj@gmail.com; we will do it within 30 days.

Children

Cutroom is not for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version is published here.
