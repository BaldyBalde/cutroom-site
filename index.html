Cutroom

Short clips from long videos, posted to your own accounts.

Cutroom is a small tool run by Jesse Turner in the United Kingdom. It cuts long videos into short vertical clips with captions, and, when you tell it to, posts those clips to your own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account.

What it does with TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, Cutroom asks TikTok for permission to read your basic profile (your display name and avatar, so the app can show which account is connected) and to publish videos to that account. It only posts a clip when you choose one and press Post. It never posts on its own, never reads your messages, followers or other people's data, and never changes your account settings.
